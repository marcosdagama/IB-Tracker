DP1 Term One Tracker

Six fortnightly sprints. Each one lists what should be acquired (can-do statements), a practice target, and a 1–4 self-check. Physics HL (Higher Level) and Maths AA SL (Analysis and Approaches, Standard Level) lead the plan (technology route); the other subjects and the Core (Theory of Knowledge, Creativity-Activity-Service and the Extended Essay) are kept on a steady, lighter track.

The fortnight cycle

Based on Zimmerman's self-regulated learning model (plan → perform → reflect).

  • P

    Plan, Monday wk 1 (15 min). Read the sprint's can-do list, block study slots in the calendar, flag anything already shaky.

  • D

    Do, both weeks. Retrieval first: close the notes and attempt questions, then check. Mix in 2–3 questions from earlier sprints each session.

  • R

    Reflect, Friday wk 2 (20 min, with a parent). Tick what is secure, rate each lead subject 1–4, write three lines, carry any 1s and 2s into the next sprint.

Self-check scale

1Can't do it yet, even with notes 2Can do it with notes or a worked example 3Can do exam-style questions without notes 4Can explain it to someone else and spot errors

Aim for 3+ on every lead-subject item by the end of the sprint after it was taught.

Confidence by sprint

Blank until rated. Two 2s in a row on a lead subject (Physics or Maths) = ask the teacher or tutor that week.

Study methods that work

From Dunlosky et al. (2013), a review of 10 study techniques.

  • Practice testing (high utility): questions, flashcards, past-paper style items. Rated most effective.
  • Distributed practice (high utility): short sessions spread over days, never one cram.
  • Interleaving (moderate): mix topics in a problem set, especially in maths and physics.
  • Re-reading and highlighting (low utility): feel productive, do little. Replace them with the three above.

Tools

  • Revision Village · AA SL Question bank sorted by topic and difficulty, with video solutions. Use it for the maths practice targets.
  • PhET simulations Free physics sims (projectile motion, forces, energy skate park) to build intuition before problems.
  • Anki Spaced-repetition flashcards for definitions, formulas and Business/History key terms.
  • Khan Academy Quick repair of algebra gaps (logs, rearranging, quadratics) when a rating is 1.
  • School platform + data booklet Whatever the school uses (often Kognity or ManageBac) and the official Physics data booklet and Maths formula booklet, used from week one.

What applies to this cohort

  • Starting the Diploma Programme in Sept 2026 means final exams in May 2028.
  • Physics uses the themes A–E syllabus (first exams 2025). Older past papers organised by old topics or options need care.
  • Maths AA (Analysis and Approaches) stays on the current guide for this cohort. A new maths guide starts teaching in Aug 2027, after he has begun.
  • History is on a brand-new guide (first teaching Aug 2026, first exams May 2028). Pre-2026 past papers won't match.
  • Extended Essay follows the 2027 guide: 4,000 words, marked out of 30, subject-focused or interdisciplinary pathway.
  • TOK (Theory of Knowledge): exhibition (one of 35 IA (internal assessment) prompts, three objects, 950 words) is usually done by the end of DP1; the essay comes in DP2.
